Answer the following questions based on the principles of wave optics and diffraction. Pay close attention to units.

Q1. Phenomenon Conditions

For a light beam passing through an aperture to undergo observable diffraction, what condition must the aperture dimension ($a$) satisfy relative to the wavelength ($\lambda$)?

Q2. Linear Width of Central Fringe

A He-Ne laser beam of wavelength $\lambda = 633$ nm illuminates a slit of width $a = 0.30$ mm. Calculate the linear width $L$ of the central bright fringe obtained on a screen placed at a distance $D = 1.0$ m away from the slit.

mm
Q3. Angular Width

Using the exact same setup from Q2 ($\lambda = 633$ nm, $a = 0.30$ mm), what is the angular width of the central bright fringe?

rad
Q4. Angular Position of a Dark Fringe

rad
Q5. Y-Ordinate of a Dark Fringe

A monochromatic light of wavelength $\lambda = 587.5$ nm illuminates a slit of width $a = 0.75$ mm. The screen is placed at distance $D = 2.0$ m. Calculate the linear position (y-ordinate) of the first dark fringe from the center.
